Get a parking permit

You can get a permit if you live on this street. Bring a bill with your name and address. A lease works too.

Fill out the short form. It takes about five minutes. You can do it online or at the front desk.

We check your papers the same day. If something is missing, we will tell you what to bring next time.

Your permit lasts one year. We will send you a note before it ends so you can renew it in time.

If you move, let us know within two weeks. We will update your permit for free.

Have a question? Call us or stop by. We are open every weekday from nine to five.

Our office is on Main Street near Oak Park. Ask for Jordan at the desk.
